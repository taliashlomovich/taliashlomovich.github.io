Photo-Stitching Workflow for Seabird Cliff Surveys

2026

I collaborated with a team of PhD students to provide a workflow for accurately counting seabirds from a dataset of survey images. Accurate monitoring of cliff-nesting seabirds is fundamental to understanding the health of marine ecosystems and informing conservation policy in UK waters. This project, recognised as the winning team, aims to improve upon traditional manual surveys by utilising computer vision software (Ultralytics YOLOv8). My role involved testing and refining our code, as well as determining the validity of hypotheses such as a uniform distribution of birds across images.
