Talia Shlomovich

I am an incoming third year PhD candidate in geometric group theory, based at Heriot-Watt University in Edinburgh. My supervisor is Matthew Cordes.

I am part of the Algebra, Geometry and Topology group at Heriot-Watt and the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training in Algebra, Geometry and Quantum Fields (AGQ CDT), with University of Edinburgh and Glasgow. This year, I am also one of the organisers of the GEARS seminar.

Previously, I completed an Integrated Masters in Mathematics at the University of Warwick, where my Master's Dissertation on the isoperimetric spectrum was supervised by Robert Kropholler. Outside of maths, I am passionate about reading, birdwatching and (very occasionally) target shooting.
